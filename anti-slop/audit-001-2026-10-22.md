# Audit loading screen

## Findings

1. **MEDIUM — R-01, R-19:** [components/loading-screen.tsx](../components/loading-screen.tsx#L92) layers radial color effects, a pulsing glow, a continuously rotating ring, and a shimmer over the logo. This conflicts with the cream, navy, and restrained-motion direction in `DESIGN.md`; simplify the loader to the brand palette and use motion only to communicate loading.
2. **MEDIUM — resilience:** [components/loading-screen.tsx](../components/loading-screen.tsx#L110) references `/logo-nijushi.png`, which is missing from `public/` in the current worktree, so the brand mark will appear broken during loading. Point it to an existing approved logo asset.

Findings 1 and 2 were approved and addressed in the loading screen.
